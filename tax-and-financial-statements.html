How provisional tax works

Provisional taxpayers pay tax in instalments during the year instead of in one amount after year end. There are two provisional payments each tax year, plus an optional third top-up payment, and each is based on an estimate of taxable income that your accountant prepares.

Annual financial statements

Annual financial statements are the formal year-end record of your income, expenses, assets and liabilities, and they are often needed for tax, banks and funders. Our compilations follow ISRS 4410 (Revised), with a checklist an accountant must complete before sign-off.
