Payroll dates and rates that matter

The EMP201 declaration and payment of PAYE, SDL and UIF are due by the 7th of the month after each payroll month. When the 7th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the deadline moves to the last business day before it.

Employers reconcile twice a year with the EMP501: an interim reconciliation for March to August, due by 31 October, and an annual reconciliation for the full tax year, due by 31 May. SDL is 1% of payroll, and UIF is 2% in total, split equally between employer and employee.

SARS can change dates and requirements, so we confirm them against SARS for every period.

Questions

Payroll services: your questions, answered

What is the EMP201?

The EMP201 is the monthly employer declaration to SARS for PAYE, UIF and SDL. It is due by the 7th of the month after the payroll month, or the last business day before that when the 7th falls on a weekend or public holiday.

What is the EMP501 reconciliation?

The EMP501 reconciles the amounts you declared and paid on your monthly EMP201 returns with your employee tax certificates. Employers do it twice a year, with an interim reconciliation due by 31 October and an annual reconciliation due by 31 May.

How many employees are included in each package?

Growth Business includes up to 3 employees, SME Full Compliance 8, Corporate Compliance 15 and Enterprise Management 25. Extra employees are billed per employee at our standard payroll rate.

Can I add payroll to Small Business Essentials?

Yes. Payroll is available as an add-on on every package, including Small Business Essentials.
