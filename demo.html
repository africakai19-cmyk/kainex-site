01 / Your dashboard

One screen, the whole financial picture

Income, expenses, net position, top accounts and recent activity — as soon as you log in. This account is on our self-service plan, so it also tracks onboarding progress and shows what still needs setting up.

Numbers here run on the same trial balance that feeds every report in the system — nothing shown here is a separate estimate.

MANAGED On a managed package, this same dashboard names your assigned Africakai accountant and shows their review queue instead of the self-service upgrade banner.

02 / Getting around

Everything grouped the way an accountant thinks

Books, sales & debtors, accounting, compliance and tools — grouped into five sections instead of one long list. Nothing renders until you open a section, so the dashboard never feels cluttered.

The same navigation shows a Trust Accounts section automatically for businesses (like law firms or letting agents) that hold client money in trust.

app.kainex.co.za/client
03 / Send your documents

Drop in a statement, PDF or CSV

Upload a bank statement PDF directly and KAINEX extracts the transactions itself, or import a CSV from your internet banking — FNB, ABSA, Standard Bank, Nedbank, Capitec, Investec and TymeBank are all recognised automatically.

You always see a preview before anything is imported.

SELF-SERVICE You upload documents yourself, whenever suits you — no waiting on a monthly cycle.
MANAGED You can still upload here, or simply send documents straight to your accountant — both roads lead to the same books.
app.kainex.co.za/client
04 / Transactions, drafted for you

AI reads every line, a person confirms it

Once a statement is in, every transaction already has a suggested account and category. Nothing books itself silently — each line is approved and posted with a single click, or flagged if it needs a closer look.

SELF-SERVICE You click "Approve & Post" yourself, whenever you're ready.
MANAGED Your Africakai accountant reviews and confirms every line before it posts — you don't have to touch this screen at all if you'd rather not.
app.kainex.co.za/client
05 / Invoice your clients

Quote, invoice, get paid — and it's already in the books

Add a client once, then invoice them in seconds. Every issued invoice posts straight to debtors and revenue — there's no separate step to bring sales into your accounts.

Below: an invoice raised against a demo client, sitting outstanding until payment is recorded.

app.kainex.co.za/client/invoices
06 / Know what's owed to you

Debtor aging, without building a spreadsheet

Every outstanding invoice is automatically bucketed into current, 30, 60, 90+ days — per client, and as a total — so chasing payment starts from a clear picture, not a guess.

app.kainex.co.za/client/reports/debtors
07 / A real chart of accounts

Proper double-entry underneath, from day one

Every KAINEX company starts with a full chart of accounts — assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses — so every transaction has somewhere correct to land, and every report is built from the same source of truth.

app.kainex.co.za/client/chart-of-accounts
08 / Trial balance, live

Books that balance — and tell you when something looks off

The trial balance recalculates from posted journal entries, not a cached number. Notice the "Unusual" flag next to PAYE Payable below — KAINEX flags accounts that move in ways that don't match a business's usual pattern, so nothing odd slips through quietly.

app.kainex.co.za/client/reports/trial-balance
09 / VAT, calculated live

No spreadsheet, no separate VAT calculation

Pick a period and KAINEX pulls output and input VAT directly from posted journal entries — not an AI estimate, the actual figures. On a managed package, your accountant reviews and files this with SARS; self-service clients can generate and check it themselves anytime.

app.kainex.co.za/client/vat
10 / Reports you can actually hand over

Profit & loss and balance sheet, one click apart

Pick any period and get a full profit & loss plus balance sheet, exportable as a PDF — the kind of report a bank, investor or SARS auditor expects to see, not a rough summary.

app.kainex.co.za/client/reports
11 / Bank reconciliation

Know exactly what doesn't tie out yet — and why

KAINEX compares your books against the actual bank statement balance and shows the difference down to the cent, with every unreconciled item listed underneath. This demo account has three items still sitting in the review queue — which is exactly why the difference below isn't zero yet. That's the system working as intended, not a bug.

app.kainex.co.za/client/reports/bank-reconciliation
