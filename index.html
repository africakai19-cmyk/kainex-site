How it works

Security & compliance

Your financial records aren't a demo

KAINEX is a processing tool under POPIA; Africakai is the responsible party. Documents and access are treated accordingly.

01

Bank statements and tax documents are stored in private buckets, served only via short-lived signed URLs.

02

Every record change keeps an audit trail — nothing is hard-deleted from a client's books.

03

Compilations follow ISRS 4410 (Revised), with a checklist an accountant must complete before sign-off.

04

Consent is captured at registration, and data breaches are reported within 72 hours, per POPIA.
