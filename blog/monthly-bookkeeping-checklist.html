KAINEX guideMonthly bookkeeping checklist: what to send your accountant

By the Africakai accounting team · 13 October 2026 · 2 min read

Accurate books depend on getting the right documents to your accountant on time. This checklist covers what to send every month, what to share once a year, and the habits that stop small problems turning into year-end headaches.

Send these every month

  • Bank statements for every business account, including credit cards and loan accounts.
  • Copies of the invoices you issued to customers.
  • Supplier invoices and receipts for everything the business paid for.
  • Slips or proof for cash expenses.
  • Payroll inputs if you have employees: new starters, leavers and changes in pay.
  • Agreements and invoices for any equipment, vehicles or other assets you bought.
  • Details of money you put into the business or took out of it personally.

If you are a VAT vendor, make sure supplier documents are valid tax invoices, because input VAT can only be claimed with one.

Share these once a year

  • Your latest SARS notice of assessment.
  • Any changes to directors, shareholders or the company's registered details.
  • Loan agreements and any new finance arrangements.
  • The previous year's financial statements, if your accountant has not prepared them.

Habits that save time

  • Send statements on the same day each month, so it becomes routine.
  • Keep business and personal spending in separate accounts.
  • Add a quick note about any unusual deposit or payment, so it is categorised correctly the first time.
  • Store invoices and receipts digitally as you receive them, not in a pile at month end.
  • Do not wait for year end. Problems found in March are far easier to fix than problems found a year later.
