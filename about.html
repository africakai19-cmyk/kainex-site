About KAINEXBuilt and run by the accountants behind it.

KAINEX is owned by Africakai, the team of accountants behind it. Every package is delivered by a qualified Africakai accountant, with KAINEX software doing the reading and drafting so nothing sits in a queue.

Owner

Africakai, a South African professional services firm specialising in compliance and management consulting.

Founded

2019, in Polokwane, Limpopo, by Madembe Maano.

Who we serve

Startups, small businesses and SMEs across South Africa, with services delivered remotely nationwide.

Who is behind KAINEX

KAINEX is the software Africakai operates for its bookkeeping, tax, payroll and compliance clients. The people who review your books, file your returns and answer your questions are the same team that owns the platform, so there is a real person accountable behind every package.

Africakai was founded in 2019 and is based in Polokwane, Limpopo. It started by helping South African businesses stay compliant with CIPC, SARS and the Department of Labour, and built KAINEX so that routine bookkeeping could be done faster without losing the accountant's check.

To learn more about the wider firm, visit africakai.co.za.

How the accountants and the software work together

KAINEX reads your bank statements and drafts the categorisation. Your Africakai accountant then reviews and confirms every line before anything reaches SARS or your financial statements. Confirmed transactions post as balanced, double-entry journal lines, so the books are always ready for an accountant to sign off.

Packages are priced by turnover, from R1,200 per month, and we confirm the exact fee before anything is signed. See the packages or the live product walkthrough.
