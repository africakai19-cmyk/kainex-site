VAT periods and deadlines

A business must register for VAT when its taxable turnover goes over R1 million in any 12-month period, and many smaller businesses register voluntarily. Most small vendors submit a return every two months, and larger vendors every month.

SARS sets the due dates for each period, and they move when a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday. We track your periods and confirm dates against SARS so a return is never filed late by accident.

Questions

VAT returns: your questions, answered

What is a VAT201?

The VAT201 is the return VAT vendors submit to SARS for each VAT period. It declares output VAT charged on your sales and claims input VAT paid on your business purchases.

Which KAINEX package includes VAT returns?

VAT return submissions are included from the Growth Business package at R2,500 per month, and in every package above it. Small Business Essentials does not include VAT returns.

Do you check my transactions before the return is submitted?

Yes. KAINEX drafts the categorisation, and your Africakai accountant reviews and confirms every line before it reaches SARS.

Can you help if I am not registered for VAT yet?

Africakai also handles SARS registrations. Message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm what applies to your business.
